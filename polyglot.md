# Polyglot privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Polyglot is a browser extension that translates web pages. It is published by Soflution Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (Companies House number 16910478). Contact: contact@soflution.com

## Short version

- Polyglot has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
- To translate a page, the text of that page is sent to the translation service you selected (Google Translate by default). Nothing else is sent anywhere.
- Your settings and preferences are stored only in your browser.
- We do not sell, rent or share your data. Soflution Ltd does not operate any server that receives your data.

## What Polyglot processes

**Text of the pages you translate.** When you click "Translate page", choose "Translate page" in the right-click menu, or when a page is in a language for which you turned on automatic translation, Polyglot reads the visible text of that page (and the text of a few HTML attributes such as title, alt and placeholder) and sends it, directly from your browser, to the translation service selected in the settings:

- Google Translate (default): translate.googleapis.com
- Microsoft Translator, if you select it: edge.microsoft.com and api-edge.cognitive.microsofttranslator.com
- Yandex Translate, if you select it: translate.yandex.net
- The translator built into Chrome, if you select it and your version of Chrome provides it: translation is done by Chrome on your device and Polyglot sends nothing over the network.

These services receive the text and return the translation. They process it under their own privacy policies. Polyglot does not send them your name, your email address or any identifier created by Polyglot. As with any web request, the service can see your IP address and the browser's standard request headers.

Pages are never translated automatically unless you turn on automatic translation for a language. You can exclude any site.

**Address of the current tab.** Polyglot reads the address of the active tab to know whether the page can be translated and to apply your per site preferences (for example, a site you excluded). Site names you exclude are stored locally. Browsing history is not collected or sent.

**Text to speech.** Where a "listen" button is available and you use it, the text is sent to the voice service selected in the settings (Google, translate.google.com, by default, or Lingva, translate.plausibility.cloud) to get the audio.

## What is stored, and where

Stored only in your browser (chrome.storage and IndexedDB), never sent to Soflution Ltd:

- your settings (target language, translation service, page translation options);
- your automatic translation choices per language and your excluded sites;
- the optional translation cache, if you turn it on (texts and their translations, to avoid translating them twice);
- the translation history and personal dictionary features inherited from the original project, if they contain entries.

You can clear the cache from the settings page, reset the settings at any time, and everything is deleted when you uninstall the extension.

## What Polyglot does not do

- No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or advertising.
- No sale or transfer of data to third parties, other than sending the text you translate to the translation service you selected, which is required to translate it.
- No use of data for any purpose other than translation, and never to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address with a new date. For any question or request, write to contact@soflution.com.
