# Tab Session Sync: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Tab Session Sync is a browser extension published by Soflution Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 16910478). Contact: contact@soflution.com

## What the extension stores

To do its single job (saving the tabs of your windows into named sessions), the extension stores:
- for each session: its name, color, creation and update dates, and the window it is linked to;
- for each saved tab: its address (URL), title, icon address and the time it was saved;
- your settings (automatic capture, badge, blank tabs).

The extension does not read the content of web pages, and does not record clicks, keystrokes, form data, passwords or your location.

## Where it is stored

Everything is stored only in your browser, in the extension's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device. A few temporary flags are kept in chrome.storage.session and are erased when the browser closes. Nothing is synced to a Google account or to the cloud: "sync" in the name means that a session is kept in step with its window while you browse.

## What is sent over the network

Soflution Ltd does not operate any server for this extension and receives none of your data. There is no account, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising.

The only network requests are made to display tab icons in the popup: the icon is loaded from the address provided by the browser (usually the website itself), and when a tab has no icon, it is requested from Google's public favicon service (www.google.com/s2/favicons), which receives the domain name of that site only. Google's handling of these requests is covered by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## Sharing and sale

Your data is never sold, shared or transferred to third parties, and is never used for any purpose other than the extension's single purpose, nor to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

## Retention and deletion

Sessions are kept until you delete them. You can delete a session or a single tab from the popup at any time. Uninstalling the extension deletes all its stored data from your browser.
