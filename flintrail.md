# Flintrail browser extension: privacy policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Flintrail is published by Soflution Ltd (United Kingdom). Contact: contact@soflution.com.

## What the extension does
The Flintrail extension lets the Flintrail MCP server running on your own computer drive the browser tabs you allow, and records a task you perform by hand so that it can be replayed.

## Data it handles
- The connection settings you enter: the local port and the token of your own Flintrail server. They are stored in the browser's extension storage on your device.
- While an agent drives a tab: the page content, URLs and the commands needed to perform your task.
- While you record a demonstration: the elements you click or change, the values you type (never the content of password fields) and the URLs you navigate to.

## Where it goes
All of this data is sent only to the Flintrail server running on your own computer, over a local connection to 127.0.0.1 protected by your token. The extension does not send any data to Soflution Ltd or to any other server, and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

Recorded tasks are saved by your Flintrail server as plain files on your computer (in ~/.flintrail/flows) and stay under your control.

## Sharing and sale
Soflution Ltd does not collect, sell, rent or share your data. Nothing is used for any purpose other than performing the tasks you ask for.

## Your control
Disconnect or remove the extension at any time from its popup or from the browser's extensions page. Delete recorded tasks by deleting their files.

## Changes
Any change to this policy will be published at this address with a new date.
